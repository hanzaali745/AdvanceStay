About Us

About TheNextGenAI

thenextgenai.com publishes practical, step-by-step guides for using AI tools — coding assistants, business and marketing tools, and productivity software. Every article aims to answer one specific question a reader actually has: how do I set this up, how do I get this tool to do X, is this worth paying for compared to the alternative.

Our Approach

Most AI tools keep their actual product behind a login, so most guides here are built from official documentation and public product pages, cross-checked against the tool's real public site rather than written from memory. Editorial Standard

How we review AI tools.

Affiliate revenue can support TheNextGenAI, but it does not replace the evidence. The tutorial and the reader's task come first.

01

Start with a real task

We choose a concrete problem or workflow rather than writing a feature list simply because a tool has an affiliate program.

02

Research current sources

Product claims, workflow steps and pricing statements must trace back to current sources. Official documentation is preferred.

03

Show the verification level

Hands-on testing, official-source verification and limited secondary-source verification are labeled differently.

04

Separate trust from commission

An affiliate link is used only when the tool is relevant to the article and useful to readers considering it.

Our rule:

A tutorial should still be useful even if the reader never clicks an affiliate link.

Our Testing Methodology

How we review and verify AI tools with transparency and integrity.

Our Approach

At TheNextGenAI, we believe in honest, transparent reviews. Every AI tool we review is tested with real-world scenarios, and we clearly mark whether claims come from official sources, our testing, or affiliate relationships.

01

Research & Learn

We start by researching the tool thoroughly, reading official documentation, understanding its capabilities and limitations.

02

Hands-On Testing

We use the tool ourselves with real-world tasks to understand its actual performance and identify what works and what doesn't.

03

Transparent Marking

We clearly label what we've hands-on tested, what comes from official sources, and which reviews include affiliate links.

04

Honest Recommendations

We recommend tools based on genuine usefulness and fit for the task, never just because of affiliate programs.
